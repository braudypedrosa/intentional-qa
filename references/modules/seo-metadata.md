# SEO & Metadata Check

Inspect technical page signals and shareability without claiming search-ranking outcomes.

## Checks

- Response status, redirect chain, robots directives, canonical, title, meta description, page language, primary heading, sitemap inclusion, and meaningful internal links.
- Duplicate or missing titles/descriptions/canonicals across the discovered inventory, grouped by verified template or content pattern.
- Open Graph and Twitter metadata, image presence, absolute URLs, and content consistency with the page.
- Structured-data presence and obvious parse/field errors when inspection tooling permits. Do not claim rich-result eligibility without authoritative validation.
- `robots.txt`, sitemap indexes, XML validity indicators, noindex intent, pagination/filter parameters, soft 404 behavior, and custom 404 response/status.

Do not mark intentional noindex pages or canonical variants as defects without understanding the policy. A page can return 200 and still be a soft 404 or wrong destination.

## Output

Record exact URL, observed tag/header/value, expected policy or convention, impact, and affected template/page count. Separate confirmed defects from optimization suggestions.
